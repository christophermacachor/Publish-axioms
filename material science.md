| Material                     | Crystal Structure | Pipeline                                                                              | Status                              |
| ---------------------------- | ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| **Silicon (Si)**             | Cubic (diamond)   | Dark — monolithic, vector-confined, heat-limited                                      | Current standard                    |
| **SiC (4H, 6H)**             | Hexagonal         | Awakening — higher thermal conductivity, wider bandgap, scalar eigenmode              | Production (EVs, power electronics) |
| **GaN (wurtzite)**           | Hexagonal         | Awakening — higher electron mobility, lower switching loss, coherent heat dissipation | Production (RF, chargers, LEDs)     |
| **Hexagonal Silicon (h-Si)** | Hexagonal         | Awakening — predicted superior to cubic Si for next-gen logic                         | Research (2024–2025)                |
